Common questions

Does TrueGrid work without signal?

Yes. Your grid reference, measuring and navigating to points all use GPS, which works with no signal. Maps need internet the first time. With Pro you can download map areas before your trip and use them offline.

How accurate is my grid reference?

As accurate as your device's GPS. The app shows the current accuracy under your grid. It's best outdoors with a clear view of the sky.

What's free and what's Pro?

Free: your live grid, the map, measuring, navigating, up to 10 saved points, and your grid on Apple Watch. Pro adds unlimited points, routes, offline maps, KML and KMZ import and export, and your points on Apple Watch.

I bought Pro but the app says Free.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then TrueGrid. You can also tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my points if I change phones?

Points are stored on your device. Restoring your phone from an iCloud or computer backup brings them back. With Pro you can also export them as a KML file and import it on the new phone.
