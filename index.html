FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01Does it work without signal?+

Yes. Your grid, measuring and navigating use GPS, which needs no signal. Maps need internet the first time, or download areas ahead with Pro.

02How accurate is it?+

As accurate as your device's GPS, usually within a few metres outdoors. The app always shows the current accuracy.

03Do I need an account?+

No. There's no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Your points stay on your device.

04Can I cancel Pro?+

Anytime, in your iPhone's Settings under Subscriptions. Or buy lifetime once and never think about it.

05Which grid formats are supported?+

MGRS, UTM, latitude/longitude in decimal, and degrees-minutes-seconds.
