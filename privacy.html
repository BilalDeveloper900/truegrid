TrueGrid Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

TrueGrid is a land navigation app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect any personal data, and we don't run a server that could store it.

Your location

TrueGrid uses your location only while the app is open, to show your grid reference, measure distance and point you to saved places. Your location is worked out on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Your points and routes

Saved points and routes are stored only on your device. If you use Apple Watch, they are copied between your iPhone and your Watch directly, using Apple's own connection between the two. Files you import or export (KML and KMZ) go only where you choose to send them.

Maps

To show maps, the app downloads map images from these free map services:

  • OpenFreeMap (Streets map)
  • U.S. Geological Survey (Topo and Satellite maps)

Like any website, these services can see your device's internet address and which map area is being loaded. We don't send them your name, your saved points or anything that identifies you. Offline maps you download are stored on your device.

Purchases

TrueGrid Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether you have Pro. We never see your name, email or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

TrueGrid doesn't collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Everything TrueGrid stores is on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
